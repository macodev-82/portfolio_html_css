Demo questions

Clear answers about this fictional experience.

These answers explain what the ClaraNest concept does and does not represent.

What is included in this demo?

This demo includes a bilingual service presentation, example service cards, a local estimate preview and educational content showing how a cleaning-business website could be structured.

Does the estimate preview send information?

No. The preview reads the three selections only in the browser, creates a temporary summary and does not send, store or submit information.

Can the experience be used in English or Spanish?

Yes. The interface, controls, navigation, metadata and explanatory content are available in English and Spanish.

Could this design be adapted for a real cleaning business?

Yes. The visual system and content structure could later be personalized with verified business information, service details and an approved contact workflow.

Does ClaraNest provide real cleaning services?

No. ClaraNest is a fictional commercial concept. It does not process service requests, provide prices or offer cleaning services.
